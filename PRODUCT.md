# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers and collaborators who want to understand Nikola Balić's tools, experiments, and writing.

## Product purpose

Show the connection between building a tool, testing it, and explaining what happened. Help visitors discover useful writing and projects.

## Brand personality

Direct, curious, exact. The user approved the personal workbench concept on September 11, 2026.

## References

SF Compute for calm navigation and explanatory visuals. PlanetScale for technical evidence. OpenAI for editorial selection. fal for confidence in color.

## Anti-references

Avoid heavy framed feeds, decorative background grids, and uppercase body text. Avoid simulated activity or invented measurements.

## Design principles

- Let actual work provide the visual character.
- Introduce the person and the practice before the full archive.
- Make articles easy to read and archives easy to scan.
- Keep all content types accessible through ordinary links.
- Preserve the site's URLs and content interfaces.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Use visible keyboard focus, semantic links, labeled forms, and readable contrast. Respect reduced motion. Keep content visible before scripts load. Support narrow viewports and a dark appearance.
