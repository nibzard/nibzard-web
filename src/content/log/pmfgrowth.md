## The myth

- What people picture: growth loops, viral coefficients, lifecycle email flows, A/B-tested landing pages
- All real — and all downstream of the actual variable
- Multiplier framing: tactics are multipliers, PMF is the base number

## The reframe

- In PLG and dev tools, growth means the product sells itself:
  - Time-to-first-value: the one metric that governs everything downstream
  - Shareable surfaces: repos, config files, APIs — the product leaves traces other developers find
  - Docs as acquisition, not as afterthought
  - Onboarding as the widest point of the funnel, not the marketing site
- Growth pre-PMF is friction removal, full stop
