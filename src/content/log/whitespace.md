## The motion

- Pick an emerging category. Enumerate everyone building in it — all of them
- Four questions per player:
  1. Who's building it?
  2. Why them — core business, adjacent expansion, or accident?
  3. Who can replicate it for free (distribution advantage)?
  4. For which customers is it build-vs-buy at all?
- The answers compose into a map with visible holes

## The pattern

- White space lives where incumbents can't go (it cannibalizes them) and newcomers won't (it's unsexy)
- Category shapes repeat: infrastructure players bundle, startups unbundle, platforms commoditize
- The map is a filter against the copy-paste landscape — people solve the problems they have, so everyone builds the same sandbox

## Perishability

- Maps now have a half-life of months; agents let you draw them in days

## Ending

- The map's value is the questions it forces, not the map itself
